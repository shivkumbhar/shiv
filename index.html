Colour guideline

Print-suitable swatch palette across all 21 SKUs. Each colour is mapped to its closest Pantone TCX reference and a CMYK approximation calibrated for offset printing on uncoated card stock — the substrate used for AUROUSA hang tags.

Print spec: Hang tags print on 350gsm uncoated kraft-faced cream card. Spot-colour Pantone is preferred over four-colour CMYK process for the swatch dome where consistency across batches matters; CMYK values listed are calibrated approximations for digital proofing only. Verify on a physical proof before production. Avoid TAC (total area coverage) above 280% to prevent ink-pooling.
