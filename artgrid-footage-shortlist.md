# AUROUSA — Artgrid Footage Shortlist by Product & Inspiration Place

Source: the 21 products and their `origin` / `story` lines in `index.html` (hang tag data).
Clips were found by searching artgrid.io's indexed pages, not Artgrid's own search, so this covers only part of the library.

**Key**
- 🇦🇺 **Confirmed Australian**: the Artgrid story or filmmaker places the clip in Australia
- ◐ **Mood stand-in**: right look, but the location isn't given or isn't Australia. Fine for texture, light and B-roll. Don't caption it with the place name.
- 🔎 **Artgrid search terms**: what to type into artgrid.io to find better clips for that place

> Licensing note: Uluru and Kakadu are jointly managed by Parks Australia with Traditional Owners. Commercial use of images of Uluru, and of some sacred sites in both parks, is restricted. Even with a stock licence, avoid clips that show the rock's sensitive faces. Use wide horizon and colour shots instead, and check Parks Australia's commercial filming guidelines before a paid campaign.

## Summary

- **Confirmed Australian:** only Robbie Fatt's footage, chiefly the *Tropical Australia Aerials* waterfall clip for **Daintree Teal** and the Blue Mountains forest clip for **Botanica**. Open his filmmaker page first, since his stories may hold more Queensland material.
- **Gaps:** Lotus, citrus close-ups (kitchen range), Byron lighthouse, Barossa vines, Uluru. These need direct Artgrid searches, or footage from an Australia-focused library.
- **Brand fit:** pick slow, uncrowded, restrained clips with place before product. Leave out logos, crowds, and anything that reads as an "Australian Made" claim.
